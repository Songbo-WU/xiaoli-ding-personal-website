Biography

Professor Xiaoli Ding is Interim Dean of the Faculty of Construction and Environment, Director of the Research Institute for Land and Space, and Chair Professor of Geomatics in the Department of Land Surveying and Geospatial Science at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. He is also Director of Key Laboratory of Geospatial Knowledge Innovation, Ministry of Natural Resources of the Central People’s Government. He received his BEng from Central South University of Technology in 1983 and his PhD from The University of Sydney in 1993. He lectured at Curtin University before joining PolyU in 1996.

His main research interests are satellite positioning, radar remote sensing, sensing technologies including distributed optical fibre sensing, geohazards, and methods and policies for land and space creation and optimal use.
