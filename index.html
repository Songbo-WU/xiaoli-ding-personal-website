01 / ABOUT

A career in
understanding
our changing Earth.

Professor Xiaoli Ding works at the intersection of satellite observation, precise positioning and the built environment.

He is Interim Dean of the Faculty of Construction and Environment, Director of the Research Institute for Land and Space, and Chair Professor of Geomatics in the Department of Land Surveying and Geospatial Science at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

His research spans satellite positioning, synthetic aperture radar interferometry (InSAR), distributed optical fibre sensing, geohazards, and methods and policies for land and space creation and optimal use. He joined PolyU in 1996 after teaching at Curtin University in Australia.

He is a Fellow of the International Association of Geodesy, the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors and the Chartered Institution of Civil Engineering Surveyors, and serves as Co-Editor-in-Chief of Remote Sensing Applications: Society and Environment.
